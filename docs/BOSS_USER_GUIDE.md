## Part A — What this system does for you (in one minute)

Think of it as **four things joined together**:

1. **Your public website** — families (employers) browse your helpers, read about your agency and send you an **Enquiry**.
2. **Your Agency Portal** — your private office. You add helpers, answer enquiries, manage hiring requests, chat with families, handle contracts and applicants, and pull reports. **Only your team can see this.**
3. **The family's own login (Employer Portal)** — where a family tracks their request, chats with you and approves a helper.
4. **AI helpers working in the background** — draft replies, screen applicants, write marketing text, read PDFs and fill forms, and prepare a 30-minute check of your work.

**The golden rule:** everything a family does on the website lands in **your Agency Portal** as a badge (number) on the left menu. Your job every day is simply to **clear those badges**.

## Part B — Logging in (and what to do when you cannot)

### B1. Getting in

1. Open your browser (Chrome is best) and go to your website address followed by **`/agency`**
   *Example: `https://your-domain.com/agency` — use the exact address your webmaster gave you.*
   *(The address `…/agency-portal` is a different page — it is the public list of agencies, not your login.)*
2. Type your **Username** and **Password**, then click the login button.
3. You land on **Home** — this is your dashboard.

### B2. The first time you log in (the 6-digit code)

If your e-mail has never been verified, the page asks for a **6-digit code**:

1. Check the inbox of the e-mail address registered to your account (**also check Spam / Junk**).
2. Type the 6 digits and confirm.
3. The code is valid for **15 minutes**. If it expires, click **Resend code** and use the newest one — older codes stop working.

### B3. Staying logged in / logging out

- Your login is remembered in that browser, so you do not type the password every time.
- **Never** stay logged in on a shared or public computer. Always use the **Log out** button at the bottom of the left menu.
- For safety, the system checks your session whenever you open a page. If it has expired it sends you back to the login screen — just log in again.

### B4. You forgot / lost your password

- If you are still logged in: use **Security** in the left menu (see Part D16) to change it yourself.
- If you cannot get in at all: this must be reset for you (ask your developer — it is a reset of the administrator login, not something you can do from the login page).

### C2. What the numbers (badges) mean

- A **red number** next to **Enquiries / Requests / Applicants List / Messages** = that many items you have not looked at yet.
- They refresh **by themselves every few seconds**.
- Simply **opening** that section clears the number — but clearing the number is **not** the same as doing the work:
  - Enquiries you still must change the **status**;
  - Requests you still must **progress**;
  - Messages you still must **reply**.

### C3. The bell icon (top bar)

The bell lists recent chat notifications with how long ago they happened. Clicking one opens that family's chat. Colours show urgency: red = URGENT, orange = HIGH, yellow = MEDIUM, blue = normal.

| Item | Public (website) | Your portal |
|---|---|---|
| Helper's name, skills, salary expectation | ✅ Visible | ✅ Visible |
| Helper's photo | ✅ **Blurred on purpose** | ✅ Clear |
| Passport number, contact number, private notes | ❌ Never | ✅ Visible |
| Unpublished (draft) helpers | ❌ Hidden | ✅ Visible |

**Do not fight the blur** — it protects your candidates. Families ask you for the clear photo, and that is exactly how you capture the lead.

## Part D — How to do every job (step by step)

## D1. Add a new helper

Screen: left menu → **Add Maid** (page title *"Add Maid Profile"*).

The form is a **wizard with 7 tabs**, always in this order:
**Profile → Skills → Employment History → Availability / Remark → Introduction → Public Introduction → Private Info**

1. Fill in the **Profile** tab (personal details, passport, nationality, salary expectation, etc.).
   - *Shortcut:* at the top there is a **PDF auto-fill banner**. Upload the helper's FDW **bio-data PDF** and the AI fills the fields for you — then **read every field** and correct anything wrong before continuing.
2. Click **Continue to Next** to move to the next tab. You unlock the tabs one at a time, so you cannot skip ahead (that is on purpose — it stops half-finished profiles).
3. **Skills** → tick what she can do (infants, elderly, disabled, housework, cooking). Add the notes the family will ask about.
4. **Employment History** → add each previous employer (country, period, duties, reason for leaving).
5. **Availability / Remark** → when she can start, off-days, interview availability, remarks.
6. **Introduction** → the internal/private introduction text.
7. **Public Introduction** → the text that appears on the public website — write it in good English, this is your sales pitch.
8. **Private Info** → passport/contact/medical-private fields. **Never put this in the Public Introduction.**
9. Add the photo(s) on the profile panel (button **Add**), then the last button changes to **Complete & Save** — click it.
10. If the reference code already exists, a message appears: **Reference Code Already Exists**. Choose **skip the duplicate** or let the system **auto-configure** it (it will use the next free number).

**Good to know**
- A save runs in the background; a small progress card shows **uploading → processing → success**. You can keep working while it saves.
- The helper stays **hidden** until you publish her (see D4).

## D2. Add helpers in bulk (spreadsheet or existing PDF)

**From a spreadsheet**
1. Left menu → **Add Maid** or **Manage Maids** → choose the **import** option.
2. Pick your Excel/CSV file. The importer understands the standard sheets: **Profile**, **Skills**, **Employment History**.
3. Check the imported rows, then publish the ones you want live.

**From a single existing PDF (foreign agency bio-data)**
1. **Manage Maids** → import → **Import External PDF**.
2. Fill the three boxes: **Full Name**, **Nationality**, **Reference Code** (the * is required).
3. Import. A note reminds you that **photos cannot be taken out of a PDF** — afterwards open the helper's profile and use **Manage Photos** to upload the photos yourself.

**Exporting instead**
- The list can export **CSV / Excel**, and any helper (or a selection) can be exported as a **PDF bio-data sheet** to send to a family.

## D3. Photos and video (the 2-photo / 1-video rule)

1. Open the helper → **Manage Photos**.
2. Click **Add** to upload. Photos are automatically shrunk so the website stays fast.
3. Use **Passport Frame → Apply Frame** to standardise the head-shot (professional look).
4. Use **YouTube Video** to add or replace her one introduction video clip.
5. Practical limit advertised to the public: **2 photos and 1 introduction video per helper**. Choose the two best photos — a clear, smiling, well-lit face shot converts best.

## D4. Publish or hide a helper

Screen: left menu → **Manage Maids**. The page starts with two cards:

| Card | What it means | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **Maids in Public** — *"View, edit or remove publicly visible maids"* | These helpers are live on the website | **Open list** |
| **Maids Hidden** — *"Manage drafts & maids hidden from public view"* | Drafts, and helpers you pulled back | **Open list** |

- On any row the action button toggles between **Hide** and **Display**.
- Newly added helpers are marked with a colourful **NEW** tag so you can spot them.

**Rule of thumb:** publish only when the passport, medicals, photo and intro are complete. An incomplete profile that a family sees is a complaint waiting to happen.

## D5. Find, edit, export or delete a helper

1. **Manage Maids** → use the **search box** and the filters (nationality, age, salary, skills, experience) to find her.
2. Click the row to open the helper's profile. The buttons there are:
   - **View All Maids** — back to the list
   - **Edit This Maid** — full edit form
   - **Full View** — large read-only view (good for showing a family on screen)
   - **Manage Photos** / **YouTube Video**
   - **Export PDF** — clean bio-data PDF for the family
   - **Bring to top** — feature her first in the list
   - **Delete** — shows **Confirm Deletion: This action cannot be undone**
3. To act on several helpers at once: tick the rows, then **Hide / Display**, **export selected to PDF**, or **delete selected** (a red confirmation appears first).

## D6. Send a helper to a family (make a proposal)

1. Open the helper → **Send to client**.
2. Pick the family (client) from the list and send.
3. The family now sees that helper inside **their own portal** and can answer **Interested**, **Direct hire** or **Reject**. Their answer comes back into your portal as a **direct sale** — watch it in **Operations Center** and in the family's chat.

**Tip:** always send the bio-data PDF **and** a chat message together. Families reply far more often when you tell them why you chose her for them.

## D7. Answer a website enquiry

Screen: left menu → **Enquiries** (red badge = new ones).

1. Open **Enquiries**. The list shows every website enquiry with the family's contact details, what they need, budget, urgency and the **Internal Note** box (a place for your team's own remarks — *"Keep the team aligned"*, the family never sees it).
2. Click an enquiry to open it and read the details.
3. Change the **status** as you work:
   **new → in progress → replied → resolved**
   - *new* = not touched yet
   - *in progress* = you are working on it
   - *replied* = you have answered / sent helpers
   - *resolved* = done, deal or no deal
4. Reply to the family either by their phone/e-mail, or open **Messages** to chat with them inside the system (recommended — it is all recorded).
5. If an enquiry is junk/spam, delete it (single or bulk). Deleted enquiries cannot be recovered.

**Boss rule:** the *Enquiries* badge must be **zero every evening**. Enquiries are the cheapest leads you will ever get.

## D8. Handle a hiring request

Screen: left menu → **Requests** (red badge = new ones).

**The four statuses** (same words your family sees):

| Status | Meaning | Set it when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for you | the request has just arrived |
| **Interested** | You found a match and told the family | you have sent one or more helpers |
| **Direct hire** | The family confirmed | they said yes — now do the contract |
| **Rejected** | Closed | they said no, or they went quiet |

**Step by step**
1. Open **Requests**. You see the overview list — *Client requests*, *What they need*, budget, agency, and a pager at the bottom (*"Page 1 of 3 · 24 total requests"*).
2. Use the **status filters** so you only see what needs work.
3. Click a request → the detail page shows **Client request**, **What they need**, **Budget**, **Request requirements**, **Client contact**, **Matched maids** and **Update status**.
4. Attach the helpers you propose to **Matched maids** (this is what the family sees in their portal).
5. Move the status forward with **Update status**.
6. Chat with the family inside the request thread (every request has its own conversation) — the family sees the same thread in their portal.
7. Housekeeping: select rows → **Delete selected** (confirm) when a request is dead.

## D9. Chat with families (Messages) + set up your chatbot

### D9a. The inbox

Screen: left menu → **Messages** — *"Messages" / "Client support inbox"*.

1. Left panel = **Conversations** with the family's name, last message and unread count.
   - **Search conversations…** to jump to a family.
   - Filters: **Status**, **Type**, and sort by **Newest first / Unread first / Oldest first**.
   - The counters at the top show **Total** and **Unread**.
2. Click a conversation — if nothing is selected you will see *"Select a conversation to begin"*.
3. Type in the box that says **"Reply to <name>…"** and send.
4. Speed up your replies with **QUICK REPLY TEMPLATES** (the panel on the right) — click one to drop the standard text into the reply box, then personalise it.

**How to use urgency (they also colour the bell notification)**
- Category: *General Inquiry, Document Request, Matching, Contract, Payment, Complaint*
- Priority: *LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, URGENT*
- Status: *OPEN / PENDING / CLOSED* — close a thread only when the matter is finished.

### D9b. Your own chatbot (so the site answers at 2 a.m.)

Screen: left menu → **Chatbot Config**.

1. **Bot name** — e.g. *Support Bot*.
2. **Welcome message template** — you can use `{{name}}` and `{{agencyName}}` and they will be filled in automatically.
3. **Fallback for short messages** and **Fallback for detailed messages** — what the bot says when it does not understand.
4. **Suggested chips** — the quick questions shown to visitors, **one per line**.
5. **Topic picker** — add a topic with its **Label, Icon, Description, Suggested message**.
6. **Response rules** — a **Rule name**, the **Keywords** that trigger it (e.g. `status, progress, update`) and the **Response** text.
7. Save, then test it by opening your own website and using the chat bubble in the corner.

**Rule:** the chatbot handles repetitive questions only. Anything about money, complaints or promises must be answered by a human — the AI is set up to draft replies for you, not to send them on its own.

## D10. Contracts and employer documents

Screen: left menu → **Contracts**.

1. The list shows every employer contract with its reference, fees and case details.
   - **Add New Contract** to create one, or open an existing one to **view or edit** it, or **duplicate** it as a template for a new family (fastest way).
2. On the contract screen the helper's data is pre-filled where possible. Fill the employer/fee/witness fields, **Save**.
3. Upload the signed paperwork — WP application draft, FDW authorisation, service agreement, pricing schedule, insurance, payslips, hand-over form. Each file is stored against that contract.
4. Open any document to **view** or **download**; delete the ones you no longer need.
5. Use **Print / Download merged PDF** to combine the whole file (contract + all attachments) into **one PDF** to e-mail or print.
6. Optional: **Generate contract draft with AI** to produce a first draft you then correct.

**Rule:** a placement is not finished until the contract exists **and** every attachment is uploaded. Your Operations screen and the AI assistant both rely on this.

## D11. Screen the people who apply to be helpers (Applicants List)

Screen: left menu → **Applicants List** (red badge = new applications).

Anyone who fills the public form **`/apply-as-maid`** arrives here automatically — with their documents and an AI score.

**The pipeline (move each person along these stages)**

| # | Stage | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New Applicant** | Just applied — nobody has looked yet |
| 2 | **Documents Submitted** | Papers received |
| 3 | **Resume Parsed** | The system has read the CV |
| 4 | **Screening Interview** | You are interviewing |
| 5 | **Background Check** | Verifications running |
| 6 | **Approved** | Passed — ready for a client |
| 7 | **Ready to Configure Public Profile** | Approved and ready to be turned into a website profile (use **Add Maid** to publish her) |
| 8 | **Placed** | Hired by a family |
| 9 | **Rejected** | Not proceeding |

**How to work this screen**
1. Open the tab **All Applicants** to see everyone, or click a stage tab to see only that stage.
2. Use the one-click **quick filters**: **WhatsApp + Score 70+**, **Childcare Shortlist**, **Elderly Care**, **Available Now**, **Ready to Configure**.
3. Save your own favourite view with **Save preset**, and pick it again next time.
4. Click an applicant to read the profile, documents, AI score breakdown, interview record and background check.
5. Move them with the stage button (**next stage** is suggested for you) — always add a short reason. Every change is recorded in the history, so you can always prove what happened.
6. Tick several people → **bulk action** to move them all at once.
7. **AI match:** paste the family's requirement text into the matching box and the AI ranks the best applicants for that job.

**Tip:** the highest-value habit here is the *Ready to Configure Public Profile* stage. Those people should become **published helpers** the same week — that is new stock for your website.

## D13. Reports — send your data to Google Sheets

Screen: left menu → **Reports** (page title *"Google Sheet Reports"*).

1. Open **Reports**. The panel at the top shows **Service account** (the e-mail that must have access to your sheet) and **Spreadsheet** (which sheet is in use).
2. If you want this agency's data in **your own** Google Sheet: paste the link into **Spreadsheet link for this agency** (the box accepts a full `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/.../edit` URL), then click **Save settings**.
3. Click **Test connection**. It tells you whether the system can reach the sheet and how many tabs the sheet already has.
4. Choose what to send:
   - **All data** — every category, or
   - **Selected only** — tick the categories you want.
5. Click **Push report to Google Sheets**. The result panel lists every tab that was written, how many rows, and a link to open the sheet.

**What lands in the sheet — one tab per category (21 tabs)**

*Agency Profile · MOM Personnel · Testimonials · Agency Staff Accounts · Maids · Enquiries · Clients · Direct Sales · Requests · Request Conversations · Request Messages · Chat Messages · Employers · Employment Contracts · ATS Applications · ATS Applicant Profiles · ATS Scores · ATS Stage History · ATS Documents · ATS Notifications · ATS Filter Presets · TikTok Integration*

*(Passwords and private keys are never exported.)*

**If it fails**
| Message | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| *Access denied (403)* | The sheet was never shared with the service account | Open the sheet → **Share** → add the e-mail shown under **Service account** as an **Editor** → **Test connection** again |
| *Not found (404)* | Wrong spreadsheet link | Paste the correct link and **Save settings** |
| *Not a valid service account key* | A setup problem on the technical side | Ask your developer to re-enter the Google key |

**Boss routine:** push this report on the **1st of every month** and read it like a scorecard — new enquiries, new requests, placements, published helpers.

## D14. The AI helpers (what to use, and what not to trust blindly)

| Tool | Where | What it is for | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Agents** | Left menu → **AI Agents** | Ask questions about **your own** data in plain English | Type things like *"show unread enquiries"*, *"find helpers for a newborn in Bukit Timah under 700"*, *"draft a reply to this family"*. It can also carry out the action it suggests. |
| **AI command center** | The floating panel (bottom of most pages) | The same assistant, always available | Click it, type, get an answer without leaving the page you are on. |
| **AI Marketing** | Left menu → **AI Marketing** | Write advertisements and social posts | Pick who to talk to → generate the campaign → copy the text into WhatsApp/Facebook/TikTok. There is also an automatic **scan** that finds good moments to advertise (holidays, slow weeks). |
| **AI HR Interviewer** | Left menu → **AI HR Interviewer** | Preliminary interview with a candidate | Start a session, let the candidate answer, then read the **score, notes and result** and decide whether to interview her yourself. |
| **TikTok** | Left menu → **TikTok** | Connect your TikTok account for automated posting | Connect → log in to TikTok → authorise. Check status any time; **Disconnect** when you take back the account. |
| **Website AI receptionist** | The chat bubble on your public website | Answers visitors instantly and captures their details as a lead | Nothing to do — it runs automatically. Read the resulting enquiry in **Enquiries**. |
| **Autopilot** | Runs by itself, every 30 minutes | Looks at your work and **prepares** suggested actions | Nothing to click. It never messages a family on its own — it leaves the suggestion for your team to approve. |

**The three AI rules for your staff**
1. **The AI drafts, a human sends.** Never let a suggestion go to a family unread.
2. **The AI is never the authority on money, refunds, fees, legal or MOM rules.** Those answers always come from you.
3. **Never paste a family's private information** (NRIC/passport, salary dispute, complaint) into the AI chat box just to "see what it says".

## D16. Security and staff accounts

**Change your own password:** left menu → **Security** → type the current and new password → save.
Do this **every 90 days**, and immediately if someone leaves or a laptop is lost.

**Adding a colleague:** staff logins are created by an administrator for your agency (the screen does not offer self-signup). Ask your senior admin/developer to add the person, and be clear about what role they should have:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Administrator (owner/manager) | Everything |
| Recruiter / staff | Day-to-day work (helpers, requests, chat, applicants) |
| Document reviewer | The document/contract side |
| Contractor | Only their own contractor task list |

**Laptop/phone hygiene**
- Always use the **Log out** button on shared machines.
- Do not save the password in a shared browser.
- Each staff member should have **their own login** — never a shared account. This is how you later know who did what.

## Part F — The rules for your team (print this part and pin it up)

### F1. Always do

1. **Set a status on everything you touch.** Enquiries and Requests are useless if they stay "new/pending".
2. **Reply inside the system (Messages), not just on your personal WhatsApp.** Then the whole office can see the conversation if you are away.
3. **Publish only complete helpers.** Complete = passport, medicals, photo, public introduction.
4. **Upload every document to the contract record** the moment you receive it.
5. **Use one login per staff member, and log out on shared machines.**
6. **Keep the phone numbers and e-mail addresses in the family's record up to date** — that is how confirmations and alerts reach them.

### F2. Never do

1. **Never share a login or password**, and never let a family use your portal.
2. **Never put a helper's passport number, medical details or private notes in the Public Introduction** (only in **Private Info**).
3. **Never promise a fee, refund, salary or timeline that the AI wrote** — check it yourself first.
4. **Never delete in bulk without reading the list twice.** Deletions cannot be undone.
5. **Never copy a helper's clear photo into a public post or WhatsApp group without her consent** — the website blurs photos on purpose.
6. **Never send marketing messages to people who did not agree to receive them** — the system keeps a consent record for exactly this reason.

## Part G — "It is not working" — what to try before you call anyone

| What you see | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| You are thrown back to the login page | Your session expired (left open too long, or you logged in on another device) | Log in again. Nothing is lost. |
| It asks for a **6-digit code** even though you logged in before | Your e-mail was never verified | Get the newest code from your inbox (also check Spam). If it expired, click **Resend code**. |
| "Invalid username or password" | Typing error, or the password was changed | Type slowly and watch the capitals. Still failing → ask your developer to reset it. |
| A red number will not go down | You **looked** at the section but the item still needs an action | The badge clears when you look; the work clears when you change the **status** or **reply**. |
| A page looks empty / old | The browser is showing an old copy | Press **Ctrl + F5** to refresh. |
| A helper does not appear on the website | She is **hidden** (draft) | Manage Maids → **Maids Hidden** → **Display**. |
| Photos look blurry on the website | Working as designed (privacy) | Nothing to fix — send the clear bio-data PDF instead. |
| An upload fails or takes forever | The file is too large, or the internet dropped | Try again with a smaller file (compress the PDF/images). |
| A report fails with *access denied (403)* | The Google Sheet was not shared with the service account | Share the sheet as **Editor** with the e-mail shown on the Reports page, then **Test connection** again. |
| An AI feature returns nothing | The AI service is temporarily unavailable | Try again in a minute, do the task manually, and tell your developer. |
| Chat messages are not arriving live | Connection hiccup | Refresh the page — the conversation history is stored, nothing is lost. |
| The whole site is down | Hosting / service issue | Check it on your phone with mobile data. If it is down there too → call your developer immediately. |

**Before calling for help, write down:** what you clicked, what you expected, what actually happened, the exact error wording, and the time. That turns a 30-minute investigation into a 2-minute fix.

## Part H — Frequently asked questions

**Q: Can a family see my other families or my profit margins?**
No. A family only ever sees their own requests, their own chat, and the helpers you send them.

**Q: Can a family download my helper's photos?**
They get the **blurred** version from the website. The clear photo and the bio-data PDF are sent by you, when you choose.

**Q: What happens to a helper I delete?**
She is removed permanently and her public page disappears. If you are unsure, **Hide** her instead — you can display her again later.

**Q: If I hide a helper, do I lose her data?**
No. Hidden helpers stay in your database (the **Maids Hidden** list) and can be shown again at any time.

**Q: Two of us work on the same request — will we overwrite each other?**
No. Chat messages and status changes are stored one at a time. Refresh the page to see the latest state before you change a status.

**Q: Does the chatbot talk to families on its own?**
It answers the website's general questions. It does **not** send promises or e-mails, and the AI inside **Messages** only **drafts** replies for your team to send.

**Q: How do I get a helper onto the public website quickly?**
**Add Maid** → finish the wizard → **Complete & Save** → **Manage Maids** → **Maids Hidden** → **Display**. She appears on the website straight away.

**Q: Where do I see website visitor numbers?**
Website traffic is measured by your analytics tool (e.g. Google Analytics), not by the portal. The portal shows **business** numbers: enquiries, requests, placements and published helpers.

**Q: Can I use this on my phone?**
Yes, the portal works on a phone. For heavy work (adding helpers, uploading documents) use a computer.

**Q: What is the difference between an Enquiry and a Request?**
An **Enquiry** is someone getting in touch. A **Request** is a formal hiring need with requirements and a status you work through. An enquiry usually turns into a request.

**Q: Do I need to tell candidates about the AI interview?**
Yes. Ask for their permission before starting an AI HR interview, and tell them a human will review the result.
