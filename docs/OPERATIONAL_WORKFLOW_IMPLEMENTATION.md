# Recruitment and Operations Workflow Implementation

## Existing foundation

- React/TypeScript frontend, a Cloudflare Worker API, and a legacy Express API used by local services.
- Supabase is the Worker-backed source for normalized operational records; the app-data blob remains for older portal data.
- Existing records include ATS applications/profiles/documents, employers and requirements, placements, placement status history, flights, arrival/medical/MOM tasks, interviews, contractor jobs, document metadata, and workflow events.
- Existing pages include applicant recruitment, employer matching, Operations Center, Operations Board, contractor tasks, employment contracts, WhatsApp broadcast, and Referral Leads.
- Make workflows are configured through server-side Worker bindings. The site can report webhook configuration; it cannot query whether a Make scenario is currently enabled or inspect Make run history.

## Implemented slices

The Operations Board now includes an authenticated interview lifecycle view. Staff can review agency interviews, update confirmation markers, reschedule or cancel an interview, and record a staff-owned outcome and notes. Updates are scoped to the staff member's agency and written to `workflow_events`. Staff can separately opt into applicant reminders during scheduling; the Worker queues 24-hour, interview-day morning, and two-hour reminders through the existing Resend integration. A Supabase outbox tracks attempts and delivery state, stale claims are recoverable, rescheduled/cancelled events are suppressed, and Resend receives a stable idempotency key. Employers can be added as interview participants after staff verifies the contact address. Once the invitation is sent, matching employer reminders are queued through the same outbox. Both audiences receive reminders only at verified/current addresses, and rescheduling or cancelling suppresses stale queued reminders. This requires `RESEND_API_KEY`, `RESEND_FROM`, the existing 30-minute Worker cron, and migrations `supabase/20261031_interview_lifecycle.sql`, `supabase/20261103_interview_employer_participants.sql`, and `supabase/20261104_interview_employer_reminders.sql`.

AI Marketing email sends are now server-side in the Worker. Full campaign drafts are stored privately per agency for 30 days; each send rechecks email opt-in, uses an agency/campaign/contact idempotency record and Resend idempotency key, and emits approved/sent/failed events without contact names, addresses, or message text. The Express marketing routes also restrict access to agency administrators, recruiters, and legacy staff roles. Apply `supabase/20261108_marketing_message_deliveries.sql` and `backend/sql/20261108_marketing_message_deliveries.sql` for the Worker and Express databases respectively. Configure Resend before sending. This records provider acceptance, not mailbox delivery.

Worker routes now apply role gates to private applicant matching, ATS review, operations board access, private placement documents, contractor task queues, interviews, and profile draft review/publication. Document reviewers may read applicant and placement records; recruiters and legacy agency staff may perform operational writes; profile publication, application deletion, and sample-placement deletion require administrator roles. Contractor task queues are staff-only because the current endpoint returns all agency tasks rather than filtering to a contractor's own assignments. Matching and operations writes require recruiter/staff roles. Express has matching route gates for its private ATS, operations, document, contract, contractor, and maid APIs. These permissions are enforced by the Worker used by the deployed site; they do not rely on hidden UI controls.

The employer requirements matching workflow now saves an idempotent Make event when requirements change and a privacy-minimized `matching.completed` event after each completed ranking. The event includes opaque employer/applicant references and scores, not employer contact data, requirement notes, or applicant names. Both the AI ranking and deterministic fallback now return up to five public, available profiles. Staff ATS approval actions emit `applicant.approved` only on a transition into Approved; placement creation emits `placement.created`, and interview outcome recording emits `interview.completed`. No event claims that identity or legal verification completed unless staff records that separately. These events use the configured deployment-task Make webhook and therefore require its scenario to accept the additional event types.

An approved Agent 3 profile draft can prefill the existing public-profile setup page's Public Introduction field. The Worker returns it only when applicant confirmation, staff approval, and active publication consent are all present. The text remains editable for staff review. The review queue now includes approved drafts and a separate Publish public profile action. Publishing requires active consent, applicant confirmation, an agency-owned maid profile with a matching applicant name, and a saved Public Introduction; the action enables public visibility, records the profile reference and staff actor, and writes an audit event. A failed workflow-state save rolls visibility back where possible. Applicant withdrawal of publication or AI-processing consent also hides the linked public profile and records the result. Apply `supabase/20261031_ats_agent_workflows.sql` before deployment.

`supabase/20261031_deployment_tasks.sql` adds an agency-scoped, placement-linked checklist table without changing existing placements or task tables. Tasks have explicit lifecycle status, assignee, planned/due times, notes, optional placement-document evidence, and verified-by/verified-at fields. The Operations Board now has a direct protected detail route per placement, auto-opens its checklist, and displays a progress timeline based on recorded task states.

The authenticated Operations Board arrival report now adds the matched applicant's name and a profile thumbnail from the agency-scoped maid record so dispatch staff can identify arrivals. These identifying details are returned only by the staff-authenticated report and are not included in the scheduled Make arrival digest. If no matching agency profile or photo is available, the report shows a safe name/photo fallback.

The legacy `/api/contracts/generate` endpoint now requires an authenticated agency user with write permission and verifies that both selected records belong to that user's agency before creating a contract record. Its returned text is a draft summary, not a generated legal PDF, approved contract, or completed signature; those steps still require the approved agency template and an authorized signing service.

The Worker initializes the checklist idempotently when a new placement is created and exposes authenticated read/initialize/update APIs. Task updates create `workflow_events` entries. Marking a task verified requires a staff verification note or a document already attached to that placement; contract generation/signature, IPA decision, insurance/bond, and Work Permit issuance tasks require a private placement document. The Operations Board provides per-placement checklist controls. The Operations Center links to that board from its Placement Steps section.

The deployment-task table was confirmed present in Supabase by a read-only REST query. The updated Worker code has not been deployed; the currently running `localhost:8080` service returned 404 for the checklist routes.

`supabase/20261031_ats_agent_workflows.sql` adds conversation, consent, confirmation, draft, and staff-review state linked to the existing ATS application. Agent 2 receives only allowlisted recruitment details and the applicant's latest message after a separate consent action. Agent 3 receives confirmed public fields only after separate publication permission. Responses are validated in the Worker, and drafts remain pending staff review; AI cannot approve or publish.

Applicants using the secure application-status token can submit access, correction, and erasure requests. The private `ats_data_subject_requests` table prevents duplicate open requests of the same type per application; authorized staff can review and record a resolution in the ATS page and workflow history. This records and routes the request but does not automatically delete data. Staff must check placement/legal holds and carry out any approved action across applicable systems. Apply `supabase/20261110_applicant_data_subject_requests.sql` before deployment; the agency still needs to approve the retention schedule that governs final erasure.

Applicant-intake, FDW application/referral, maid-referral sheet sync, placement milestone, contractor-arrival, interview-scheduled, daily rolling-arrival, document upload/verification, verified deployment-task, external Google Drive/media outcomes, and marketing campaign draft events use the durable `make_webhook_deliveries` outbox, stable event IDs, `x-idempotency-key`, bounded retries, stale-claim recovery, and a 30-day terminal-record retention window. Marketing draft events contain campaign identifiers, goal, audience type, and eligible-contact count only; they contain no recipient identities or message content. The deployed Worker marketing audience API now reads per-channel opt-in state from `public.consents`, records documented opt-ins, records withdrawals (including legacy WhatsApp marketing consent), and fails closed when Supabase consent storage is unavailable. Without Supabase consent storage, campaign generation returns no deliverable contacts. A scheduled flight now plans the airport-pickup deployment task for its arrival time; rescheduling refreshes that deadline, while verified pickup work is reopened only when the planned arrival actually changes. Scheduled/completed arrival events also enter the outbox. Google Drive reports that Make accepted the sync request; media transcription reports completion only after receiving a non-empty transcript. Neither event contains a file path, signed link, transcript, filename, or contact details. Contract generation/approval/signature, manually recorded MOM status, medical, SIP, arrival, work-permit issuance, and handover events use task IDs and emit their specific event only when staff verifies that checklist step. The outbox stores the hook key rather than the secret URL, is inaccessible to browser roles, and is visible to agency staff through Technical Settings without showing payloads. The existing 30-minute Worker cron processes retries; the first delivery attempt runs immediately after the event is persisted. Apply `supabase/20261105_make_webhook_outbox.sql`, `supabase/20261106_expand_make_webhook_outbox.sql`, and `supabase/20261107_expand_make_webhook_outbox_marketing.sql` in order; configure `MAKE_WEBHOOK_URL_DEPLOYMENT_TASK_EVENTS` and `MAKE_WEBHOOK_URL_MARKETING_CAMPAIGN_EVENTS` as needed. Agent 2 and Agent 3 webhook calls remain synchronous so the applicant can receive a reply; they use stable payload-hash idempotency headers, retry once for transient network/timeout/rate-limit/server or malformed-JSON failures, and log failed/invalid-contract events without storing messages. They are not suitable for deferred delivery because the current UI awaits their result.

The Operations Board can upload placement documents into the private Supabase Storage bucket, list metadata scoped to the placement, issue a short-lived signed download URL, and attach a document to a deployment task as verification evidence. It accepts a limited set of common document/image formats up to 25 MB; these uploads are not sent to Make.

The Operations Board includes structured staff-only MOM/IPA tracking per placement: linked applicant and employer IDs, submission date, MOM reference, IPA status, decision evidence, approval date, rejection reason, assigned staff, and notes. Only a private placement document can be attached as IPA evidence. An Approved decision requires evidence and an approval date; a Rejected decision requires evidence and a reason. Final decisions synchronize the existing IPA checklist task and audit history; an approval event is queued to Make only when staff records the approved state. This is manual status recording and does not submit to MOM or make legal decisions. Apply `supabase/20261101_placement_mom_application_tracking.sql` before deployment.

Employer accounts can now open a Placement documents page in the client portal when the authenticated account email exactly matches the placement's employer contact email. The API independently checks that ownership for listing, upload, and short-lived downloads. Employer uploads are private placement documents and move the employer document receipt task to `VERIFICATION_PENDING`; they do not mark documents as verified. Staff can open each listed document from the Operations Board using the staff-scoped signed-URL endpoint. The task states distinguish not requested (`NOT_STARTED`), requested (`PLANNED`), uploaded/review pending (`VERIFICATION_PENDING`), verified (`VERIFIED`), rejected (`REJECTED`), and resubmission required (`RESUBMISSION_REQUIRED`). Apply `supabase/20261109_deployment_task_resubmission_status.sql` before deploying this state option. An upload after a prior verification reopens that review task. The client portal account must use the employer email stored in the agency record.

The Operations Board can now add placement-scoped flight records, update status, and reschedule arrival time. It also shows an agency-scoped rolling three-day arrival report from the existing `flights` table (today and the following two Singapore calendar days); cancelled flights are omitted. At 08:00 Singapore time, the Worker can POST the report to the server-configured `MAKE_WEBHOOK_URL_ROLLING_ARRIVAL_REPORT`, with retries at 08:30 and 09:00. It uses a stable daily event ID and KV delivery state. Make must deduplicate by event ID and configure the operations recipients.

The Operations Center KPI strip now loads agency-scoped applicant, employer, placement, enquiry, interview, arrival, and deployment-task counts from `/api/operations-board/summary`. Task cards are explicitly labeled as open checklist work; they are not evidence of a real-world MOM or IPA decision. Scheduled arrival reports also require `ROLLING_ARRIVAL_REPORT_AGENCY_ID` and include that agency ID in their idempotency key and payload, preventing a shared webhook from receiving flights across agencies. Set the binding to the agency served by that deployment before enabling the scheduled report.

Agent 1 referral leads now use `public.ats_referral_leads` as the Worker-backed source of truth when Supabase is configured. Existing agency KV lists migrate lazily and idempotently on the first referral read or write; subsequent list, import, consent, and interest updates persist agency-scoped rows in Postgres. The existing 5,000-record cap and duplicate-phone checks remain in place. Referral leads can be imported from CSV/XLSX or from a bounded range in the agency's saved Google Sheets reporting spreadsheet. The sheet reader runs server-side with the configured service account; the page accepts a tab/range (for example `Referrals!A1:Z501`) and routes rows through the same validation, duplicate, and consent handling as file imports. The broadcast endpoint now verifies consent using the saved referral record or active Supabase consent ledger, not just the browser's spreadsheet claim. Staff opt-in and WhatsApp START/STOP update the shared ledger and referral record; a KV opt-out marker remains as an immediate suppression fallback. Apply `supabase/20261113_ats_referral_leads.sql` before switching an environment to this path. KV remains a local/legacy fallback only when Supabase storage is not configured. Google Sheets import also requires valid per-agency spreadsheet configuration and server-side service-account credentials.

The Contractor Tasks page can assign placement tasks to an active contractor belonging to the agency. It issues a seven-day secure link; only a SHA-256 token digest is stored. Contractors can acknowledge the assignment and later report completion through that link. A completion report becomes `COMPLETION_REPORTED`; staff must review it and enter a verification note before marking the task complete. Both events are audited and sent to the deployment-task Make outbox without names, notes, or contact details. Staff can rotate the link and copy it to share directly with the assigned contractor. The link does not send a message automatically; configure an authorized contractor notification channel before automating delivery. Apply `supabase/20261102_contractor_assignment.sql`, `supabase/20261101_contractor_self_service_acknowledgement.sql`, and `supabase/20261112_contractor_completion_report.sql` before deploying these APIs.

`supabase/20261101_arrival_dispatch_details.sql` adds structured arrival terminal, pickup contact, transport, accommodation, and special-instruction fields. Apply this migration before deploying the updated flight APIs. Keep those fields limited to authorized dispatch recipients in the Make scenario.

This applicant-agent slice requires applying the ATS workflow migration and configuring the server-only `MAKE_WEBHOOK_URL_AGENT_2_PROFILE_COLLECTOR` and `MAKE_WEBHOOK_URL_AGENT_3_PROFILE_BUILDER` secrets. Interview reminders and employer invitations also require `RESEND_API_KEY` and `RESEND_FROM`. No production deployment or Make scenario activation was performed.

## Agent naming clarification

The prompt's Agent 3 is employer-to-applicant matching; that workflow already exists in the Operations Center Match Helper. The separate imported Make blueprint called Agent 3 Profile Draft Builder prepares an applicant's employer-facing profile text. Keep these as distinct functions in the website and deployment configuration.

The employer Match Helper now captures household size, number of children and elderly members, cooking requirements, and preferred start date alongside the existing salary, availability, language, nationality, and experience fields. These values are stored on the existing employer-requirements row and included in the matching context. Unreported household counts remain null so a blank field is not misrepresented as zero. Apply `supabase/20261114_employer_household_requirements.sql` for the Worker-backed Supabase database; apply `backend/sql/20261114_employer_household_requirements.sql` if the legacy Express database is still used.

Confirm migration compatibility against the target database first.
2. Complete staging end-to-end tests for import/deduplication, consent and opt-out, Agent 1–3 responses, profile approval/publication, employer matching and selection, interview invitations/reminders/confirmation, employer document upload/review, contract review/signature recording, manual MOM/IPA updates, dispatch acknowledgement/completion, medical/SIP, work-permit recording, and verified handover. Include duplicate webhook, delayed/cancelled flight, rejected document, reschedule, and expired-link cases. These flows have not been proven end-to-end in the deployed environment.
3. The Make webhook URLs and scenarios are external configuration. Confirm every required scenario is enabled, provider connections work, webhook schemas match, retries deduplicate by event ID, and the operation recipients are configured in Make run history. The website cannot inspect Make's active toggle or successful delivery history beyond its own outbox.
4. No DigiSign provider credentials/API contract are available in this project. Contract checklist and staff review are present, but real e-signature sending/status callbacks are not integrated. Direct MOM/ICA APIs are also unavailable here; staff must use authorized official systems and record verified outcomes.
5. The agency must approve retention periods and legal-hold rules before automatic applicant erasure or private-document deletion can be safely implemented. The system currently routes data-subject requests for staff review and limits terminal Make outbox records to 30 days.
6. Confirm an approved contractor notification channel and recipient/contact policy before delivering reports or task links automatically. The application can generate the rolling report and secure links, but link sharing currently remains a staff action; do not send applicant identity details to an unverified contractor destination.

Keep MOM and legal decisions staff-controlled; do not present a webhook response as a verified real-world completion.
